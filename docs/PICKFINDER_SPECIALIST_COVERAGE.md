# PickFinder specialist coverage expansion

Official free research directory: https://www.pickfinder.app/free-research . Specialist labels are observations, not independent statistics. Native/custom single-select combinations on observed profile tabs remain bounded; custom-custom combinations, multi-selects and undocumented widgets are not certified. Individual prices retain isolated book badge, source scope, observation time and explicit threshold when available. Missing threshold is never inherited from fantasy offer. Reviewed identity/event crosswalk and two fresh matching sides are required for shadow comparison. Adapter is not yet connected to live model training. Locked models unchanged. Read-only Pick Builder and local alert limitations remain. Full coverage and live acceptance are open.

Public directory crawler follows allowlisted, same-origin links, including visible all-event tier research and player pages. It resumes from digest-named archived snapshots and refreshes research pages daily and profiles weekly. Budget: 80 requests per execution, one-second pacing; scheduled runs continue remaining discovered pages. Counts are not proof of all directories or filter states. Deep archive reports a feature matrix per sport including missing fields; a label observation never certifies semantic correctness or model acceptance.
