# NBA and esports research parity with NHL

Owner requirement, 2026-11-25 UTC: apply reusable NHL research and validation methods to NBA, CS2, Valorant, League of Legends, Dota 2 and COD. This is a permanent development requirement, not a claim that the six systems are ready. Do not copy hockey-specific coefficients, rules or statistical exposure units. Keep NHL and MLB intact.

| NHL method | NBA equivalent | Esports equivalent | Current acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Official player-game identity and complete final stats | Native NBA player/game IDs, positive participation, official final box scores | Organizer roster and permanent player ID, exact completed map/series, native final stats | Partial; ESP-017/046/049 |
| Historical source completeness and zero-stat reconciliation | Appearances, minutes and explicit zeros, no DNP inferred from missing rows | Map ordinals, substitutions, overtime, forfeits, pauses and stat completeness | Partial |
| Expected ice time and per-minute rates | Expected minutes and per-minute productivity | Expected rounds (CS2/Valorant) or minutes (LoL/Dota/COD) and per-unit productivity | Retrospective paired workload experiment submitted; actual pre-event workload evidence still required |
| Opponent shot/concession and role context | Opponent pace, position/role, lineup and usage context | Opponent strength, map/mode, role, roster, patch, side and tournament context | Same-opponent retrospective experiment submitted; verified live contextual inputs pending |
| Injuries, line assignments and goalie status | Official availability, starting lineup, minutes/role changes | Confirmed roster, substitutes, map veto and tournament format | Partial; no inference of participation from absence of injury |
| Multiple fixed historical baselines | Last 10/30, distribution scores and workload comparison | Per-title, map-scope and ruleset-specific historical comparison | ESP-053 and ESP-054 |
| Calibration and uncertainty | Chronological CRPS, interval coverage, group/event cluster comparisons | Same metrics per title and map scope, distinct series clusters | Retrospective only; independent calibration pending |
| Correlation research | Within-player residual dependence, shared-game/team effects | Within-player residual dependence and shared-map/series effects | Within-player diagnostics implemented; cross-player joint dependence not verified |
| Immutable pre-event model revision and source archive | Actual pregame records with fixed version and time | Actual pre-series records with verified map scope and fixed version | ESP-050/051; not certified |
| Independent final grading and daily health check | Official final source reconciled separately from collection | Native organizer final source reconciled separately from collection | ESP-046/049/052; scheduled evidence pending |
| Champion/challenger and untouched evaluation | Separate December 1 UTC registered forward cohort | Preregistered future independent series cohorts | Do not promote based on historical or synthetic tests |
| Site transparency | Coverage, source timestamp, completeness and errors | Separate status for every title and metric | Pending live acceptance |

The acceptance standard is source evidence plus reproducible tests and actual independent results. A historical experiment cannot prove prospective accuracy, and missing data must not be reported as a successful capture. Preserve the zero-paid-credit restriction and existing access controls. Do not add automated wagering functionality. Maintain master roadmap task IDs and record evidence at each completed scope.
