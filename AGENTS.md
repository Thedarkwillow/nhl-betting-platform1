# Betting platform repository instructions

- This public repository (`Thedarkwillow/nhl-betting-platform1`) is the default target for new development, testing, automation and the website. Keep the legacy private repository and the existing MLB repository intact.
- Never publish credentials, private/restricted raw data, or licensed feeds without verified redistribution rights. Use repository secrets for authorized credentials. Never print secret values.
- Preserve the zero-paid-credit restriction unless the owner explicitly changes it. Keep genuinely prospective results separate from historical replay, with independent validation and abstention when evidence is incomplete.
- Do not promote unqualified models or place wagers. Test isolated public code before merging. Preserve existing dashboard and legacy private-source workflows until validated replacements are available.

## Mandatory roadmap maintenance
- Before each sprint, read the public [master roadmap](docs/MASTER_BETTING_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md), which preserves the full original history and IDs, and when applicable the legacy master roadmap at https://github.com/Thedarkwillow/nhl-betting-platform/blob/main/docs/MASTER_BETTING_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md. New development belongs in the public repository; preserve the private master and its history.
- In every development sprint reconcile completed work against the actual task acceptance criteria. Check off scoped completed milestones only after successful tests, merge and real evidence where required. Record the UTC date, stable task ID, commit/PR and successful workflow URL; explicitly record partial, blocked, pending, or failed acceptance. Tests passing alone do not prove live collection, model improvement, app availability or deployment.
- Add newly identified work as unchecked tasks, do not silently delete tasks, never mark a broader parent complete from a narrower milestone, and reopen checked items if evidence is invalidated. Update the public master roadmap and completion evidence before reporting completion. If a post-merge run is necessary, use a follow-up evidence-only update once verified.
- Every PR description should state affected task IDs and acceptance evidence or why roadmap status is unchanged. Final sprint report must identify what was actually checked off and what remains open.

## NBA and esports research parity
- Apply reusable NHL data-quality, workload, opponent/role context, statistical validation, provenance, immutable archival and independent grading standards to NBA and every supported esports title. Follow [NBA and esports NHL research parity](docs/NBA_ESPORTS_NHL_RESEARCH_PARITY.md); adapt sport-specific statistics and never transfer hockey-specific assumptions or certify unverified sources. Keep separate evidence per sport/title and maintain the master roadmap.

## PickFinder-first research and spending policy
- Owner directs that PickFinder Premium be the primary research collection priority across enabled sports and esports: extract individual book-specific offers, board metrics, player statistics, historical logs, line movements and scoped matchup/context data where available and authorized. Preserve source, observation clocks, period/map/mode, missingness, and independent quality checks. This is a research priority, not automatic first-party verification or permission to promote model inputs.
- Retain existing direct feeds, API integrations and official statistical sources for independent corroboration, settlement, missing data and fallback. Do not disable working collectors or assume PickFinder covers disabled or empty sports. Do not purchase additional API subscriptions or increase paid usage until the available Premium data has been extracted and evaluated and the owner explicitly approves any change.
- Prioritize secure persistent authentication, actual private exports, completeness reconciliation, separately gated 6/9 AM Pacific captures and verified archival before adding new providers. Keep credentials out of Git and public logs; no access-control bypass. Preserve locked models and use challenger validation before promoting new inputs. Track acceptance under ESP-062/063 in the master roadmap.

- Collection priority: NBA, NHL and NFL first, then esports with independently verified upcoming competition (including CS2, Dota 2, Valorant, LoL and COD whenever active). Keep unknown/offseason titles queued for historical research and status rechecks; do not infer inactivity from failures. Use weighted deep-profile sessions to avoid starvation. No extra paid discovery.
