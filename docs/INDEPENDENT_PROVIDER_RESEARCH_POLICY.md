# Independent provider research policy

Reviewed 2026-11-24: https://prizepicksapi.com/terms and https://underdogapi.com/terms permit product display subject to their terms and prohibit redistribution of raw feeds or bulk exports outside granted rights. Keep provider keys server-side. Public repository contains source and aggregate status only; raw capture, commercial display scope and retention require documented entitlement review. Attribution is displayed near provider metadata.

Coverage and price quotes are documented zero-credit operations. Credit-plan compact projection pages cost 1 per requested 100 rows; full pages and exports cost more (PrizePicks 3 per requested 100). See https://prizepicksapi.com/credits and https://underdogapi.com/products/projections. Legacy requests are not credits. Never assume an account quota from a marketing page.

The independent adapters accept live JSON only. A provider observation is not original-platform confirmation. No automated metered collection is enabled by this policy. Before a bounded live test, obtain remaining quota, confirm the request's cost and permitted retention, and explicitly approve a budget. Do not publish raw responses or keys. Missing or stale information remains missing; no demo record is genuine evidence.
